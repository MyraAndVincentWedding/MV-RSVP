Our Story

A greater story together,
one moment at a time.

01

The Beginning

I first met Myra in our hometown when she joined the Little Ms. Bagasbas pageant. At the time, she was actually competing against my sister.

I remember seeing her smile during the pageant, and for me, that was enough.

It was love at first sight.

I was always shy around her back then. We were young, and neither of us really made the first move. We simply knew each other, crossed paths, and went on with our lives.

But somehow, that little moment stayed with me.

02

The Journey

Eventually, we found our way into each other's lives.

We had a common friend, and I asked him if he could help me get the chance to talk to Myra. It wasn't some grand beginning. It was just a small step that eventually led us here.

Our first real moment together was a road trip. Somewhere along that drive, I started to feel that what I was feeling for her was becoming something serious.

Then came the late-night drives, the random trips, and the nights when we'd go all the way to Sipocot just to see what we called the “stars.”

They weren't actually stars. They were the lights along the road, reflected through all those pavement markers.

But they were our stars.

Through every high and low, every misunderstanding, and the problems we each had to face, we continued choosing to be there for each other. We didn't always have everything figured out, but somehow, we always found our own way through it together.

With Myra, I found something I never knew I needed so much:

comfort.

The kind where I can express myself without worrying about being judged or misunderstood.

And even something as simple as eating together became special.

It was ordinary.

But with her, ordinary moments never really felt ordinary.

03

The Moments

There are memories I know I'll always carry with me.

The late-night road trip to Sipocot.

The “stars” that weren't really stars.

The random moments when we would simply go out in the middle of the night without needing a reason.

And, of course, the moments when Myra asks me about dates I should absolutely know by now… and somehow, I still don't know the answer.

But one of the biggest things I've learned about us happened during one of the hardest moments of my life.

This year has been one of the hardest yet most blessed years for us. So much happened in such a short amount of time, especially after I asked her parents for their blessing.

Even when things felt difficult, God continued to bless us and redirect our path.

And on one of my worst days, Myra was still there.

She accepted me. She cared for me.

That was one of the moments I knew I could see myself spending my life with her.

Because love isn't only about the best days.

It's about knowing that someone will still be there on the worst ones.

And sometimes, all it takes is a surprise hug from Myra to make everything feel a little better.

04

The Yes

I proposed to Myra in Kiudkad, Siruma, on the day of our prenup shoot.

And the funny thing is, I had already made sure she wouldn't expect it.

I wanted her to believe I wasn't going to propose that day. I told her I didn't have savings yet, so she wouldn't expect a proposal.

Before that moment, everything seemed completely normal. We were simply preparing for our prenup shoot.

Then I told her how I felt.

I kept the message short. I told her how much I loved her and that I would continue loving her until the end.

And then I asked her.

For a moment, it looked like her mind was still trying to understand what was happening.

Then she started crying.

And while crying, she pointed at the ring in her hands and told our friend:

“Meron na akong singsing.”

That moment still makes me smile.

For me, it was a strange mix of every possible feeling. Happiness, nervousness, excitement, love, and everything in between.

And after all the tears, we went right back to our prenup shoot.

Laughing.
Enjoying the day.

And now, that unexpected moment has become one of the memories that brought us here.

05

The Next Chapter

When I look at Myra now, I don't just see the person I'm going to marry.

I see home.

Myra is home.

I see a future filled with love and respect. A healthy family that grows together, makes memories together, and looks back on those memories years from now.

There are still so many places we want to see, so many things we want to experience, and so many dreams we're working toward.

And honestly, I'm looking forward to simply enjoying life together.

Resting together.
Eating together.
Travelling together.
Growing together.
Building our future together.

I don't know exactly what every chapter ahead will look like.

But I know who I want beside me through all of it.

I promise to do everything I can to make us happy.

And Myra, if I could tell you one thing on our wedding day, it would be this:

I'm not the one Heaven sent. You are the angel who guided me toward this amazing life I have today.

Our story started in Bagasbas, with a smile during a little pageant.

It grew through road trips, late nights, ordinary meals, unexpected moments, difficult days, and countless memories.

And before I asked you to be my wife, I first asked your parents if I could court you.

Maybe that's why “A Greater Story Together” feels so right.

Because our story began the right way.

And now, we're ready to begin the greatest chapter of it together.

Same journey. Always.

- Vincent
